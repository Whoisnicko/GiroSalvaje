# Stop Humans! v21-alpha.3 — Sorcerer casting prototype

Adds a separate magic module: 100 MP, 2 MP/s simulated combat time, cooldowns, wave reset; Q or Cast button, one continuous stroke (4 real seconds), recognized rune then target selection (2 real seconds), absolute 0.3× combat speed during both states, three-second real global recovery after cancel/cast. Invalid or locked gestures/targets do not spend mana. Visual trace, target ring and preview confirmation only; NO damage, protection, statuses, XP or permanent unlocks. Campaign Fire only; all six rune previews in sandbox, infinite mana and per-spell cooldown bypass independently toggled, refill; global recovery retained. Mana/cooldowns save with campaign; transient gesture does not. Existing cards, Sanctuary art and practice preserved. Syntax, DOM IDs, simulated campaign/sandbox cast, invalid target, resource, timing, reset, toggles and phase tests passed. Browser and Cloudflare deployment unverified.
