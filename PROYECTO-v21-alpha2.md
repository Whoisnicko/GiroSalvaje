# Stop Humans! v21-alpha.2 — Sandbox foundation

Disposable state from main menu, ordinary relic choice. Campaign parked and restored on exit; sandbox excluded from saves, score and campaign card rewards. Replenished finite counters displayed as infinite souls/spins; all cards reusable; direct permanent guardian levels 1–3, custom enemy queue including commanders, wave selection, heal and optional god mode for allies/altar, stop/repeat and clear. Temporary summons via existing spells/necromancer. Capacity cap 24, manual enemy cap 60, repeated Howl guard 48; physical placements still limited. Collapsible panel above field. No spellcasting, mana, permanent magic, or Sanctuary changes. Syntax, DOM IDs, resources, cards, enemy scaling and campaign refill guard tested. Browser and deployment pending.
