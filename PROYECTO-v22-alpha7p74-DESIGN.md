# V7.74 — Guardian Ascension (design proposal, not runtime)
Date: 2026-11-28. Baseline: production v22-alpha.7.73, commit a4903620c0ec67fe52b49708980854753c6ddcbb. User authorized production promotion and design, not implementation of the numbers below.

## Audit of current implementation
engine/game.js: BASE / MERGEABLE / LV_MULT / LV_CD / LV_RANGE / stats / bansheeOn / update / damage / hitEnemy / landShot. Five mergeable guardians W/A/M/N/B. Tier III x10 base HP/damage, x0.7 attack interval, x1.2 range except Banshee. Banshee III +80% damage and attack rate (strongest aura wins), radius 220. Dragon already has 70 base splash; Wraith orb single target. Necromancer raises eligible corpses with cap 6 per wave at rank III; skeleton actual rank stored in nl, temporary and wave-scaled. Werewolf is temporary rank I, II/III art only. Existing modifiers include training, infusions, artifacts, Warlord, biome cover/blizzard/ash and Cathedral mitigation. Do not conflate projectile traversal with armor bypass (damage option pierce). Ordinary attacks and special attacks must respect existing applicable mitigation, no separate hero spell classification.

## Proposed initial tuning (all provisional)
- Zombie III — Plague Colossus: every 8 simulation seconds, replace next valid melee hit with normal hit plus disease to primary and up to 2 nearby living enemies within 45 logical px of primary. Disease total 30% of that attack's effective pre-mitigation damage over 3 seconds per target (3 ticks of 10%), 15% movement slow on non-bosses; boss damage allowed, no slow. Refresh duration only, strongest source wins, no tick duplication; no extra hit knockback or leech from ticks. Green impact/ooze.
- Wraith III — Soul Reaper: every 6 seconds, replace next valid orb with a straight spectral blade following initial aim, using current effective ranged reach, collision half-width 10 logical px plus enemy radius. At most three distinct enemies in travel order, 100/45/25% of that normal effective attack, armor/cover still apply, no splash, no double hits, no normal orb on same attack. Projectile travel-time and ash crossing respected. Cyan crescent and trail.
- Dragon III — Frostfire Breath: every 9 seconds, replace normal fire projectile with a frostfire projectile/impact. Normal splash and damage inside existing effective splash radius (70 base), radius extended by 25%; up to 3 extra enemies in outer ring get 35% of normal damage. No second normal projectile, no repeated primary damage, no freeze by default. Blue flame cone as casting VFX and expanded impact.
- Necromancer III — Army of the Dead: every 12 seconds, next eligible normal corpse raises two Tier III skeletons instead of one if two remaining slots under existing total cap of 6 per wave; if only one slot, regular raise, special not consumed. Same corpse processed once, no extra souls, no resurrection loop. No new spawn without corpse; secondary spawn uses valid nearby ground. Purple/green dual summoning circles.
- Banshee III — Death's Lament: every 12 seconds while at least one valid enemy is within current aura radius, apply -15% outgoing damage for 4s to up to 5 nearest living enemies; bosses -7.5% for 2s. Strongest effect only, refresh not stack, no extra ally buff beyond current +80%. Sound wave and debuff symbol.
- Skeleton III (nl=3) — Bone Guard: proposed passive one-time shield of 15% max HP on creation, no refresh/recast, removed at wave end; optional separate balance gate because Necromancer already receives a special. Shield should reuse verified shield/damage pipeline, not overwrite stronger shields. Werewolf II/III remain non-playable until separate evolution design approved.

## Cross-cutting rules
Only rank III, no base stat modifications. First readiness after 3 simulation seconds, then listed cooldown from successful activation (no cooldown consumption without valid opportunity). Normal attack cadence remains; specials replace attacks, except support effects. Cooldowns independent of Banshee, Haste, Warlord, speed artifacts and attack-rate synergy. Simulation time scales with game speed and time-slow mechanics; no wall-clock timers. Do not activate while dead, frozen, in reserve or outside fight; existing projectiles/valid disease may finish after source death. No multiplicative stacking from multiple guardians for same status. Route damage through existing hit/damage and biome systems; explicitly test Cathedral shield, armor, forest cover, frozen blizzard, ash projectile crossing, boss immunities. Validate no reward duplication, no leech from DoT, no extra knockback spam. Keep old saves compatible: persist bounded cooldown/status state or deterministic safe initialization, reset wave/sandbox appropriately. Respect reduced motion and VFX budget. Data registry ASCENSION_RULES, separate engine/guardian-ascension.js; adapters in existing engine paths and sandbox readiness display/optional reset only in test environment.
