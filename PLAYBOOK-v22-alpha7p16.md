# Playbook — Responsive UI v22-alpha.7.16
Production: https://stophumans.pages.dev/
GitHub: https://github.com/Whoisnicko/StopHumans (main)
1. Export existing save as a precaution. Ctrl+F5, verify version. Use browser zoom 100% for first check.
2. Continue a campaign at native resolution; inspect HUD, gacha, cards, reserve, shop, guide and map. Test both windowed and fullscreen (F11); resize to a smaller window and return. Ensure no horizontal page overflow on desktop and no clipping of primary actions.
3. Drag a guardian onto the field and back, click a card, cast/aim a spell, buy a spin and Undo after resize. Verify souls, army and campaign are unchanged by resizing.
4. Open Hades Room, Grimoire, bestiary, tutorial and route map; ensure they fit or scroll internally and all controls are reachable.
5. Test actual OS display scaling and browser zoom preferences. Report monitor resolution, OS scaling, browser zoom and screenshot if layout needs tuning. Small screens retain scrollable layout; ultrawide intentional centered margins.
Next: Encounters & Route Economy only after playtest approval.
