Guided exercises hide sandbox control rows and quick magic targeting; God mode and unlimited resources are intentional for learning. No new assets, no change to campaign balance, save schema or approved HUD layout. Training panel only visible in Sandbox. Parked campaign Undo state is now restored on exit.
Validation: local Chromium with inline assets/fallbacks and simulated localStorage. Real button clicks, pointer dragging, triangle trace and Warlord movement/skill verified all six exercises; exit preserves campaign snapshot (excluding autosave timestamp), profile and Undo; Free Sandbox works.
