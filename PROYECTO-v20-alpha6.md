# Stop Humans! v20-alpha.6

Profane Sanctuary inspector anchored bottom-left of battlefield for hover and pinned selection. Preparation Undo: up to ten deterministic actions (sale, movement, reserve, stack/merge, held symbols, extra spin purchase, field slot, prep card, stolen artifact). Restores unit data, original drag positions, soul balance, relevant card/altar/artifact/capacity fields. Resets on spin, random card purchase, combat, wave/run replacement, or resize. Session-only, not persisted; restored game state autosaves after undo. Ctrl/Cmd+Z or Undo under battlefield. No new HUD art yet; no combat changes. Syntax and isolated state/position tests passed; browser/deployment unverified.
