# Playbook — Encounters & Route Economy
Production: https://stophumans.pages.dev/
GitHub: https://github.com/Whoisnicko/StopHumans (main)
1. Export your save, Ctrl+F5, verify v22-alpha.7.17. Continue older campaign to confirm unchanged rules; use New Game to test actual node rewards.
2. Win wave 1. Map offers two destinations with exact benefit. Pick, reload at arrival, select Prepare next wave. Reward granted once only; reload again and compare souls/army/altar.
3. Vault: +3 souls, Bloodfang costs 6 instead of 8; scroll prices unchanged. Forge: +3 souls, Bone Armor costs 6 instead of 8. Buy then Undo; actual discounted price restored. Discounts end at battle start.
4. Crypt: previewed rank-1 guardian appears on field or reserve, otherwise +3 souls. Shrine: +6 souls. Sanctuary: +3 souls, restore up to 15 altar HP (never above max).
5. Check even-wave blessing/card remains, free spins/hand/army recovery and normal Hades access remain. Try both heroes and windowed/fullscreen. Report which routes felt compelling, whether souls were too abundant, and if any route felt useless. Next iteration: adjust prices/benefits and consider merchant-only access after evidence.
